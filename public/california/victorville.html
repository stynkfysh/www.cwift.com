Homes in Victorville

Old Town Victorville, along D Street and Seventh Street on old Route 66 near the Mojave River, has the city's oldest homes, many small and from the early and middle 1900s. Spring Valley Lake, an unincorporated HOA community just east of the river, is built around a private lake and golf course. Much of the city is 1980s to 2000s single-story and two-story stucco tracts on block-walled lots, with newer subdivisions spreading west toward Highway 395 and north toward Adelanto. Manufactured homes and larger rural lots sit at the edges, and desert landscaping often replaces lawns.

What moves home values in Victorville

Many newer west-side tracts carry Mello-Roos taxes, and Spring Valley Lake has association dues and lake rules; the report lists each from the tax bill and HOA records.

Spring Valley Lake homes on the water or the golf course sell differently from interior lots, so the report compares sales with the same kind of frontage.

Block walls, RV parking, pools and desert landscaping are compared with similar Victorville sales, and larger rural parcels at the edges are matched with similar acreage.

Small early-1900s and mid-century houses in Old Town vary widely in upkeep, so clear photos of roofs, wiring and kitchens weigh heavily in the condition rating.

Each of these is documented from your photos, permits and records and reflected in the comparable-sales adjustments.
