Privacy

How Cwift handles what you send.

Cwift is operated by Brian Ward (Brian Ward Appraisal). This note explains what happens to the information you send through this website.

What we collect

The details you enter in the order form or include in an email (your name, email address, the property address, the purpose and dates of the appraisal, and any notes) and the photos or documents you upload or attach.

How it is used

Only to evaluate, invoice, prepare and deliver your appraisal and to answer your questions. Appraisers must keep a workfile for each assignment under USPAP, so information relied on for a report is retained as part of that workfile.

Where it goes

Form submissions are delivered by email; uploaded photos are stored in a private cloud folder used only for appraisal files. We don't sell or share your information, except as required by law or by appraisal standards (for example, a regulator's review).

Cookies and analytics

The site uses no advertising cookies. Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses to protect the site from abuse and to count visits in aggregate.

Questions

Email contact@cwift.com.
