24-Hour Appraisals for Trust and Estate Administration

Certified values for trustees and executors: accountings, distributions and trust sales.

In plain English

If you're the trustee or executor, you need a value for the house that you can show the family, the court or the CPA, and explain where it came from. This is that value.

A trustee or executor is expected to know what the trust or estate holds, what it is worth, and how that number was reached. A desktop appraisal gives you a signed, certified value you can put in an accounting, use to balance distributions, or show a beneficiary who asks where the figure came from.

Administration runs on other people's schedules

A beneficiary meeting is set for Monday. A buyer wants to close. The CPA needs values to finish the fiduciary return. When the real estate value is the last open item, 24-hour delivery takes it off the list.

Common administration uses

  • Valuing the residence on the date of death for the trust's records and the beneficiaries' basis.
  • Current value for a distribution in kind, or for a sale of the home to one of the beneficiaries.
  • Balancing shares when one heir keeps the house and the others receive cash.
  • Supporting the real estate figures in a trustee's accounting.
  • Setting the asking price before the trust lists the property.
  • A private, fully reasoned appraisal next to the probate referee's inventory value, when the CPA wants narrative support for the heirs' basis.
  • The real estate value on the Inventory and Appraisal when the court waives the probate referee under Probate Code §8903.

What to send

  • The valuation date or dates you need: date of death, current, or both
  • The trust or estate name as it should appear on the report
  • Photos and any records of the home's condition
  • The intended users, such as co-trustees, the attorney and the CPA

Is a desktop appraisal right for this?

Desktop appraisals suit most administration tasks, particularly when a fiduciary needs a defensible number quickly and the property's condition is well documented. In a formal California probate, real estate on the inventory is normally appraised by the court's probate referee, and a private appraisal can still support pricing, distributions and the heirs' tax basis. The personal representative can also ask the court to waive the referee for good cause (Probate Code §8903). The petition attaches a proposed inventory and appraisal, the hearing is set at least 15 days out, and the referee may oppose it. If the court grants the waiver, an independent appraisal can supply the real estate value for the inventory.

If co-trustees or beneficiaries are already at odds over value, consider an inspection-based appraisal, and ask your attorney how a private appraisal fits your case.

Questions

Do I need an appraisal to settle my parents' trust?

Usually, when the trust owns a house. Trustees use the value to divide shares fairly, to sell the home to a beneficiary, to price it for sale and to document the beneficiaries' tax basis. Your trust attorney can confirm what your trust requires.

Can we skip the probate referee and use our own appraisal?

Sometimes. Under Probate Code §8903 the court may waive appraisal by the probate referee for good cause. The personal representative files a petition with a proposed inventory and appraisal and a statement of the good cause, and the hearing is set at least 15 days later. Courts don't grant every request, so ask your probate attorney first. For comparison, the referee's commission is one-tenth of one percent of the value appraised, with a $75 minimum and a $10,000 cap (Probate Code §8961).

Can I get the date-of-death value and today's value at the same time?

Yes. Both opinions of value can be developed in one assignment if you ask for them when you order. The fee and the delivery time are confirmed on your invoice before you pay.

Who is allowed to rely on the appraisal?

The client named in the report and the intended users listed in it, typically the trustee, the attorney and the CPA. Name everyone who needs to rely on it when you place the order.
