Homes in San Mateo County

Daly City's Westlake and the hills of Pacifica hold postwar row houses and ranch homes in the fog belt. Along the bay side, San Bruno, Millbrae, San Carlos and Belmont climb from 1940s-1960s tracts into hillside homes with bay views, while Hillsborough, Atherton and Woodside are estate towns on large, wooded lots. Foster City and Redwood Shores were built on bay fill from the 1960s on, with lagoons, townhomes and condos. The Coastside, from Montara and Moss Beach to Half Moon Bay and Pescadero, mixes older cottages, newer homes and farmland.

What moves home values in San Mateo County

Coastal bluff erosion in Pacifica and parts of the Coastside affects value and insurability; distance to the bluff edge, any city notices and coastal-zone permit limits are weighed against nearby sales.

Foster City, Redwood Shores and other bayfront neighborhoods sit on fill behind levees; flood-zone status, insurance needs and any settlement or foundation issues are checked against similar sales.

Woodside, Portola Valley and the hills west of Interstate 280 bring wildfire exposure, septic systems and steep driveways; lot photos, access and permit history inform the comparison.

Each of these is documented from your photos, permits and records and reflected in the comparable-sales adjustments.

Courts, the assessor and deadlines in San Mateo County

San Mateo County probate cases are heard in the Superior Court of California, County of San Mateo, and Redwood City is the county seat. The San Mateo County Assessor handles Prop 19 parent-child exclusion claims and Prop 8 requests for a decline-in-value review. Owners seeking a formal hearing had a 2026 regular assessment-appeal filing period that was set for July 2 to November 30. Each filing pins value to a particular day, whether a date of death, a transfer or January 1, and a Cwift report is written to that day.

Questions from San Mateo County

How do you appraise my late mother's Pacifica house near the bluffs in San Mateo County?

The appraisal starts with sales along the same stretch of Pacifica coast, matched for bluff distance, ocean view and lot condition. Photos of the yard edge, retaining walls and drainage help, along with any geologic reports or city letters about the bluff. If erosion has limited insurance or repairs, that is noted, because buyers on the San Mateo County coast price those limits in.
