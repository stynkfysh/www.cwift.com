About Brian Ward and Cwift

Cwift is the 24-hour desktop appraisal service of Brian Ward Appraisal, run by a California Certified Residential appraiser who has valued homes since 2004.

The appraiser

Every Cwift report is researched, written and signed by Brian Ward, California Certified Residential Real Estate Appraiser (license AR036053, issued by the California Bureau of Real Estate Appraisers). Brian has appraised California homes since 2004 and completed more than 7,000 appraisals. He has served as an expert witness in litigation, bankruptcy and family law matters and reviews other appraisers' work for attorneys. No trainees or outside appraisers work on Cwift files.

Why Cwift exists

Brian's date-of-death practice delivers desktop appraisals on a standard schedule. Clients kept arriving with deadlines that couldn't wait a week: a closing, a tax filing, a mediation, a court date. Cwift, a play on the word “swift,” was created for them. It is the same report from the same appraiser, delivered within 24 hours, seven days a week.

How the work gets done

  • Statewide MLS, assessor and recorder data, the same sources any California appraiser uses
  • Development and reporting under USPAP, with a signed certification on every report
  • Client photos and records to describe the home, disclosed in the report
  • A workfile retained for every assignment, and free revisions if the property was misdescribed
