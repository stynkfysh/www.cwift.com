24-Hour Appraisals for California Property Tax Appeals

Independent market value evidence for Prop 8 reviews and assessment appeals.

In plain English

If you think the county values your home too high for property taxes, an independent appraisal is the evidence you bring to the assessor or the appeals board.

When a home's market value falls below its assessed value, Proposition 8 allows a temporary reduction. If the assessor won't agree, the county assessment appeals board hears the evidence, and an independent appraisal is the kind of evidence boards are used to weighing.

The filing window closes on a fixed date

Regular appeal applications are filed between July 2 and either September 15 or November 30, depending on the county (State Board of Equalization). Appraisals ordered in the final days of the window are where 24-hour delivery earns its keep.

Where an appraisal helps

  • Prop 8 decline-in-value requests to the assessor.
  • Formal assessment appeals before the county board or a hearing officer.
  • Questioning a base-year value set after a purchase, an inheritance or other change in ownership.

What to send

  • The assessor's parcel number and the assessed value you are contesting
  • The date to be valued: January 1 of the tax year for a decline-in-value case, or the date of the ownership change
  • Photos, especially of anything that hurts value, such as deferred maintenance

Send photos with your order through the upload page, or attach them to a reply to your confirmation email.

Is a desktop appraisal right for this?

Appeals boards decide on evidence of market value, and a well-supported desktop appraisal presents the same comparable-sales evidence as an inspected one.

If your county or hearing officer prefers a particular report format, ask before you order.

Questions

How do I lower my property taxes in California?

If your home's market value on January 1 was below its assessed value, ask the county assessor for a Prop 8 decline-in-value review. If that doesn't work, file an appeal with the county board during the filing period. An independent appraisal of the January 1 value is the evidence.

Will an appraisal guarantee a lower assessment?

No one can promise that. The appraisal provides independent evidence of market value, which the board weighs against the assessor's evidence.

Which date should the appraisal use?

For a decline-in-value appeal, January 1 of the tax year in question, the lien date. Tell us the year and the effective date is set to match.
