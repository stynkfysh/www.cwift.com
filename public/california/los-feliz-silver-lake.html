Homes in Los Feliz / Silver Lake

This stretch runs from the Los Feliz flats east through Franklin Hills to the Silver Lake Reservoir. Franklin Hills has hillside cottages and modern houses on narrow streets joined by public stairways, and the Shakespeare Bridge crosses one of its ravines. Around the reservoir, hillside homes include modernist work by Richard Neutra and Rudolph Schindler alongside 1920s Spanish and Craftsman houses. Hyperion Avenue and Griffith Park Boulevard carry bungalow courts, duplexes and small apartment buildings, and newer small-lot homes and condos cluster near Sunset Junction.

What moves home values in Los Feliz / Silver Lake

Reservoir and hill views: homes looking over the Silver Lake Reservoir, downtown or the Griffith Park ridges sell above enclosed lots, and the appraiser confirms the view from photos and the lot's orientation.

Small-lot homes: newer small-lot houses near Sunset Junction share driveways and maintenance agreements, so the appraiser compares them with other small-lot sales rather than older detached houses.

Modernist houses: intact work by Neutra, Schindler or their contemporaries attracts buyers who pay for original design, so the appraiser seeks architect-designed comparables and notes alterations from photos.

Busy boulevards: houses fronting Hyperion, Fountain or Silver Lake Boulevard carry more traffic than quiet hillside lanes, and comparables are matched for street type.

Each of these is documented from your photos, permits and records and reflected in the comparable-sales adjustments.

Questions from Los Feliz / Silver Lake

Can an appraisal support a Prop 8 review on my Silver Lake condo?

It can. If your Silver Lake condo's market value on January 1 fell below its assessed value, the Los Angeles County Assessor can lower the assessment temporarily under Prop 8. An appraisal dated to January 1, using sales in your building and nearby complexes, gives the assessor clear evidence. The outcome isn't guaranteed, but documented sales carry more weight than estimates.
