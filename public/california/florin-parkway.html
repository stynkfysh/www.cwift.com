Homes in Florin / Parkway

Housing here is mostly single-story ranch homes from the postwar decades, on flat lots along streets off Florin Road, Mack Road, Stockton Boulevard and Franklin Boulevard. Many have stucco or wood siding, attached garages and deep back yards, and some sit on larger parcels left from Florin's farm days. Valley Hi and the blocks near Center Parkway are mostly tract neighborhoods. Duplexes, fourplexes and manufactured home parks mix in along the main roads, and Morrison, Elder and Florin creeks cross the area.

What moves home values in Florin / Parkway

Flood exposure along Morrison, Elder and Florin creeks: parts of the area have a history of creek flooding, so the FEMA zone for each address is checked and noted in the report.

Garage conversions and back-yard units are common, and the appraiser separates permitted from unpermitted space using city and county permit records and photos.

Condition varies widely from street to street, so interior photos decide whether a house compares with fully updated sales near Florin Road or with ones still needing work.

Location along busy corridors such as Florin Road, Stockton Boulevard and Mack Road, or near the light rail line, is compared with similar locations rather than quieter interior streets.

Each of these is documented from your photos, permits and records and reflected in the comparable-sales adjustments.

Questions from Florin / Parkway

Does the granny flat behind my mom's house in Florin count in the appraisal?

It can, if it was built or legalized with permits. The appraiser checks Sacramento County or city records, looks at photos of the unit's kitchen, bath and separate entrance, and compares the property with south Sacramento sales that also have a second unit. If the unit is unpermitted, the report says so and treats it more cautiously, usually as contributing less than a legal one.
