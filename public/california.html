24-hour desktop appraisals anywhere in California

All 58 counties, from the Mexican border to the Oregon line. Coverage doesn't depend on driving distance, so a Modoc County cabin is as close as a Los Angeles condominium.

Statewide by design

Location changes the comparables, not the timeline

Desktop appraisals draw on the same statewide data every California appraiser uses: MLS records, county assessor and recorder data, and sales history. Location affects which comparable sales exist, not whether the work can be done. Thin rural markets sometimes need a wider search, and the report explains how the comparables were chosen.

  • Single-family homes and condominiums in every county
  • Current and past valuation dates
