What to include in your email

  • The property address
  • The purpose, and who will rely on the report (CPA, attorney, trustee, court)
  • The date the value is needed for: today, a date of death, a separation or transfer date
  • Your deadline
  • Photos, attached or as a shared link
  • Which report you'd like: 24-hour or standard turnaround, Standard or Basic
